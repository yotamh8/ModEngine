# ModEngine Terms of Use and Acceptable Use Policy

Version 1.0.0 - 2026

ModEngine is a tool for changing values in **single-player games you own, while you play them offline** - more
health, more money, slower time, a teleport - the same kind of thing a game's own cheat codes or debug menu
would do. These terms explain what it is for, what it must never be used for, and who is responsible for what.
They are part of the ModEngine License (EULA). By using ModEngine you agree to both.

## 1. What you may use ModEngine for

- Single-player games, and the single-player or offline modes of games, that you have legally obtained.
- Your own experiments, learning and accessibility needs in those games (for example easier difficulty,
  slower game speed, or skipping a section you can't physically play).
- Programs you wrote yourself or have permission to inspect.

## 2. What you must never use ModEngine for

1. **Online play of any kind**: multiplayer, co-op with other people, online matchmaking, ranked modes,
   leaderboards, tournaments, online events, or any game while it is connected to its online services.
2. **Games protected by anti-cheat, and getting around anti-cheat, DRM or copy protection.** ModEngine has no
   features for hiding from anti-cheat, and anti-cheat systems detect it. It warns you when it sees anti-cheat
   running in a game, but it does not stop you: if you go ahead, any ban or other consequence is yours alone.
3. **Unlocking anything that is sold**: paid DLC, in-game purchases, premium currency, subscriptions, season
   passes, memberships, or creator/supporter tiers (for example Patreon-locked content). ModEngine refuses to
   read or change values that look like purchase or membership checks.
4. **Gaining an advantage over other people**, or affecting anyone else's game, account, items or experience.
5. **Taking modified progress online**: do not upload, sync or use saves, characters, items or currency that
   were changed with ModEngine in any online mode. Many games ban accounts for this.
6. **Touching programs you have no right to touch**: other people's computers or accounts, work or school
   software, security software, or anything outside games.
7. **Anything illegal**, or anything that breaks the terms of service or license agreement of the game or

## 5. Changes in game folders

For some engines ModEngine adds small, removable helper files: for Godot games, one line in the game's
`override.cfg` (removed when you choose "Forget this game"; an existing file is backed up and restored). For
Unity and Ren'Py games, a helper is loaded into the running game's memory - nothing is written to the game
folder, and it is gone when the game closes. ModEngine never changes a game's own program files.
